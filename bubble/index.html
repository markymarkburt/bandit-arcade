How to play

Move your mouse (or drag) to aim, click or tap to fire. Bubbles stick where they land — group 3 or more of the same color to pop them. Anything left hanging with no connection to the ceiling falls too. Clear the board to win; if the bubbles reach the bottom, it's over.
