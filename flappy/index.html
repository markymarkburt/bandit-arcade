How to play

Click, tap, or press Space to flap. Gravity pulls the Bandit down; each flap gives a little lift. Thread the gaps between the towers — every tower you clear is a point. Clip a tower or the ground and the run's over. The gap tightens as your score climbs. Best score is kept for the session.
