How a hand works

Heads-up Hold'em, simplified: you and the Bandit each get two cards. Blinds are 10/20 and rotate each hand. Bet, call, or fold before the flop, on the flop (3 community cards), the turn (4th), and the river (5th). If nobody folds, the best five-card hand at showdown wins the pot. Bets are a fixed 20 — the Bandit calls with decent hands, folds trash, and occasionally bluffs. Bust the Bandit to win.
